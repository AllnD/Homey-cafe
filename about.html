WHY FRIDGE BISTRO?

A proper meal,
without the hassle.

Every day, we have to make the same decision: What are we going to eat? Fridge Bistro was created around a simple idea: I call it the 4th alternative: keep a proper meal in your freezer and make dinner whenever you want it. The "4th alternative" provides real value in terms of opportunity cost-your time, effort and money. For working adults, the value isn't simply in the price of the bento. It is what you can do with the time you dont spend deciding what to eat, preparing ingredients, cooking and cleaning up.

Fridge Bistro isn't trying to replace cooking, restaurants or food delivery. Sometimes you want to cook. Sometimes you want to go out. Sometimes you just want someone else to bring food to your door.

YOUR MEAL OPTIONS

What's for dinner?

Every option has its place. Here's what a typical meal actually involves.

Cook

TRADITIONAL
  • Plan way ahead. Decide what to eat, what ingredients to buy
  • Prepare ingredients on the day of cooking
  • Cook
  • Wash dishes during and after cooking, wash dishes after meals
  • Before eating, wash up while the food becomes cold
  • 45–60 minutes
Pros

Freshly prepared and complete control over ingredients.

Cons

Time, preparation and cleaning.

GrabFood

DELIVERY
  • Browse restaurants
  • Decide what to order
  • RM20–40+ food
  • Delivery fee
  • Wait 30–60 minutes
  • Variable quality
  • Possible delivery delays or problems especially on rainy days
Pros

Huge selection and no cooking or washing up.

Cons

Waiting time, more expensive, delivery fees and variable experience with food quality.

Eat Out / Takeaway

GO OUT
  • Decide which restaurant
  • Dress up
  • Drive or walk out
  • Variable costs
  • More time needed for the whole journey
  • If taking away, food might not be hot
Pros

Enjoy the restaurant experience and freshly prepared food.

Cons

Travel, time, cost and the extra effort involved.

THE IDEA

Keep good food waiting for you.

By using food science and careful freezing techniques, we design our menus to stay as fresh, tasty, and textured as possible. To help you unlock those original flavors at home, how you reheat your meal is key. Check out our simple guide below to get started!

Microwave: Microwaves heat food from the outside in by vibrating water molecules. This creates instant steam within the food.Fast and efficient, but it can dry out uncovered food or turn delicate greens mushy if blasted for too long

Steamer: Steamers surround food with gentle, moist heat at around 100°C, helping to retain moisture and keep food tender during reheating.Transfer the frozen meal into a suitable heatproof container or steaming dish before steaming. Choose a relatively flat container to help the steam circulate around the food and promote more even heating..

Air fryer/oven: These appliances circulate high, dry heat around the food. Dry heat removes surface moisture, making fried coatings crispy again
