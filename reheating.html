FRIDGE BISTRO

Reheat. Serve. Enjoy.

Your meal is ready when you are. Follow the reheating instructions below to enjoy your Fridge Bistro meals at their best.

Before reheating: Keep frozen meals frozen until you are ready to reheat. Standard microwave is 800W. The instructions here are guidelines for your particular meal.Please adjust time and technique accordingly based on your own reheating equipment. Remove packaging that is not suitable for your chosen reheating method. Use suitable steaming or oven plates for reheating.

IMPORTANT: For microwave, the lid should be used to cover the food loosely which leaves some small gap for steam to escape. For steaming, use the parchement paper supplied to cover the top to prevent water from dripping down and diluting the taste of the food. Make sure the food is thoroughly hot throughout before eating.

MENU 01

Nasi Lemak with Malaysian Curry

This menu has 3 items. The main bento container, curry chicken round container and a small packet of roasted peanuts and anchovies. Both containers can be reheated via microwave and or steaming. The peanuts and anchovies should be stored at room temperature. Open the plastic when using.

Steaming ✓

  • To take frozen items out of the container, twist or squeeze opposite sides of the container to loosen the contents. Place frozen content into steaming plate.
  • Use provided parchment paper to cover the items to prevent water from dripping onto food
  • Steam for up to 25 to 30 minutes

Microwave ✓

  • Place main meal container into microwave, place cover loosely on top with some gaps for steam to escape. Reheat at HIGH for 6 to 8 minutes
  • Place container with curry chicken into microwave, losely place lid on top with some gaps for steam to escape. Reheat at HIGH for 4 to 6 minutes

Air Fryer / Oven ✕

  • Not suitable

MENU 06

Chicken Chop with Apple Sauce

This menu has 6 (A, B, C, D, E, F) items which can be reheated using separate different methods. Airfryer and oven to reheat item A & F will be best for preserving the intended texture and taste of this menu.

Thawing ✓

  • Item D= Bread : Leave at room temperature to thaw for 10 minutes

Steaming ✕

  • Not suitable

Microwave ✓

  • Item A = Chicken chop and baked potatoes: Microwave at HIGH for 5 to 6 minutes
  • Item B=baked beans : Microwave at HIGH for 2 minutes
  • Item C= Mixed vegetables: Microwave at HIGH for 2 minutes
  • Item E= Apple Sauce & Carrot soup : Microwave at HIGH for 2 minutes
  • Item F= Apple Pie : Microwave at HIGH for 1 minutes

Airfryer/Oven ✓

  • Item A = Chicken chop and baked potatoes : Airfry on 180'C for 10 minutes. For oven, reheat at 180'C for 15 minutes
  • Item F = Apple Pie : Airfry on 180'C for 10 minutes. For oven, reheat at 180'C for 15 minutes
